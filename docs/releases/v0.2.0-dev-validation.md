# 0.2.0 development validation

Checked on Windows with Godot 4.7.2 .NET, an AMD Radeon RX 7900 XTX, .NET 8 client libraries and the .NET 10 runner/tests. This records local development verification, not a published release or a hardware support guarantee.

The full test run preceded the final context-only follow-ups. Those follow-ups cover mandatory-model gating, rejected-plan atomicity, stale interventions, literacy and family context, elections/unions, world persistence, generated layouts, regional material costs, physical cargo and narration. The additional family-context test brings current discovery to 74 cases; a second complete long-run sweep was not performed.

## Live model

The local main role used Qwen3.5-4B, `UD-Q4_K_XL`, through llama.cpp b12020 with Vulkan. Two slots share a 32,768-token context allocation. No helper or embedding service was needed.

A seed-42 world with one band, 25 people and 160 × 80 cells advanced 32 simulated days. Three councils were accepted at days 0, 15 and 30; eighteen personal reflections were recorded. The final relationship-aware check rejected three invalid layout/community proposals, retried without applying them and completed in 60.2 seconds. Twenty-five people remained alive, two windbreaks were complete and band food nutrition was 516. These figures describe one short run, not long-term balance or a deterministic model response.

Earlier runs of the same duration accepted three councils without retries. Supplying explicit hunger/food feedback changed subsequent work towards food production. Model output remains variable; valid replies are not a guarantee of wise decisions.

## Visual review

I inspected the live client and exported game captures. Specialist fixtures initially disappeared behind their room floor; open-room draw order now puts the floor behind independent objects. Applying appearance settings now preserves natural garment colours unless a dye is selected.

Local review files in `build/review` include `specialist-interior-fixed.png`, `character-model.png` and `standalone-outfit.png`. They are review captures, excluded from source and game packages. No new artwork family was generated; existing palette and asset IDs remain unchanged.
