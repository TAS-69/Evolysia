## Live model

The local main service used Qwen3.5-4B `UD-Q4_K_XL` through llama.cpp b12020/Vulkan, with two slots and 32,768 total context tokens. No helper or embedding service was needed.

A primitive 32-day check accepted three councils and rejected one proposal, retaining all 25 people. A developed saved settlement then ran two further days with the expanded production/service/utility/road contract: one council accepted, two proposals rejected, 42.5 seconds elapsed and 25 people alive. The rejected proposals double-booked a worker and placed a chest outside a room interior; neither applied world changes.

The accepted developed-world council chose empty production/service/road order lists and disabled fuel use with a full battery reserve. This proves the contract, correction loop and continuation, not useful autonomous industrial management. Staffed services and manufacturing effects are instead verified by the integrated settlement tests. Longer generative economic acceptance remains open.

## Vegetation rendering follow-up

Foreground vegetation was darkened by sampling its texture twice in the pixel shader. Its seasonal colours were also rounded to eighths, unlike the terrain shader's 8-bit blend. The shader now applies the original vertex tint once; foreground palettes use the same 8-bit seasonal blend with rounded channels.

The actual GPU comparison initially failed all 48 cases, with a maximum channel difference of 66. After the fix all 48 passed, with at most one 8-bit channel value of difference. Cases cover trees, shrubs and rocks, 16/32/64-pixel artwork and wet/dry plus autumn/winter blends. The client builds without warnings/errors; artwork validation still passes all 3,614 entries. No sprites or asset IDs changed.

I inspected `build/review/vegetation-fixed.png`. The isolated regression scene is documented in the building/testing wiki and excluded from exported resources. The local Windows package was rebuilt; ZIP CRC passed.
