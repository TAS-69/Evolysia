# Evolysia 0.1.0-alpha.1

The first playable alpha: generate a planet, seed its peoples and watch what follows. Divine intervention is included. Good judgement is sold separately.

## Play

Download **Evolysia-0.1.0-alpha.1-windows-x64.zip**, extract the entire folder and launch `Evolysia.exe`. Keep its data folder, `assets` and `Evolysia.pck` together. The .NET runtime is included. Use a Vulkan-capable GPU on Windows x64.

## Included

- Four planet types, seeded world previews and manual or scattered placement of one to six peoples.
- Individual needs, inheritance, emotions, fear learning and relationships; emerging cultures, faith, inventions and conflict.
- Gathering, farming, herding, material processing, owned gear, settlement construction and sea migration.
- Planet-to-settlement zoom, modular outfits, regional building styles, roof cutaways and representative storage contents.
- Seventeen powers, including dreams and rare messages, plus inspection, chronicle, overlays and time controls.
- Optional locally hosted voices and pivotal choices. The ordinary simulation needs no model server.
- Official logo in the menu and startup splash; the default Godot splash image is replaced.

## Before you start

**There is no save/load.** Worlds are session-only, and starting a new world replaces the old one.

Container inventories are quantity-based and their visible contents are representative. Full cargo logistics, direct unit control, multiplayer, a player-facing outfit editor, complete specialist institutions, magic and space gameplay are unfinished. Some detailed assets remain unintegrated, and some sprites enlarge base artwork at close zoom. Higher simulation speeds depend on population and hardware.

Windows x64 is the initial packaged target. Other platforms and GPU configurations have not been verified. The screenshot capture path reports resource cleanup warnings at shutdown; see [validation](v0.1.0-alpha.1-validation.md) for the exact checks and limits.
