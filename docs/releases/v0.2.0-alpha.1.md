# Evolysia 0.2.0-alpha.1

The generative alpha. Every band is now run by a council of its own people, voiced and decided by a language model, and nobody hands them a manual. They learn what their work and buildings do by doing it, and they find new techniques by experimenting, sometimes brilliantly, sometimes by burying meat to see what happens.

## Play

Download **Evolysia-0.2.0-alpha.1-windows-x64.zip**, extract the entire folder and launch `Evolysia.exe`. Keep its data folder, `assets` and `Evolysia.pck` together. The .NET runtime is included. Use a Vulkan-capable GPU on Windows x64.

**A running model is required.** Install Python 3.11+ and start the local service from the extracted folder with `python tools/llm_lab/lab.py bootstrap --helper off --embed off`, or use **Start model** in the game. The first start downloads the server runtime and model weights. You can also point **World → Model connection** at another compatible endpoint. Time waits while the model is unavailable or thinking.

## New since 0.1.0-alpha.1

- **Councils decide.** Each band's council plans work, personal goals, construction, migration, sharing, diplomacy, leadership, divisions and unions, manufacturing, services, power, roads and cargo. People remember their reasons. Invalid proposals change nothing.
- **Discovery by experiment.** Councils see only their band's lore:
  - traditions;
  - what each kind of work has actually brought, including whether it can be eaten;
  - what their buildings and products did;
  - the techniques they know;
  - clues and dead ends.

  New techniques come only from experiments they devise, an action such as heat, strike, weave, plant or tame on things they can perceive. Near misses leave clues; knowledge can die with the people who hold it.
- **Saves.** F5 quick save, F9 named slots and a save browser, and three rotating autosaves.
- **Industry and infrastructure:**
  - manufacturing orders that stage real inputs;
  - fuel and solar power with batteries;
  - staffed care, teaching, research, worship, governance and guards;
  - roads that need clearing and materials;
  - physical stock with cargo jobs.
- **Building:** specialist interiors and generated building layouts.
- **New powers:** terrain shaping, plant growth and basic possession of one adult.
- **People up close:** portraits with inherited features, an outfit editor (clothing must still be made), settings, and new fonts.

## Before you start

This is an alpha. A slow model makes time slow, and small local models sometimes misread their own lore or repeat a dead end. Development saves may not load in later versions. Magic, space travel, complete late-game production, laws and markets, full logistics and some artwork are unfinished; see [Development status](https://github.com/TAS-69/Evolysia/blob/main/docs/DEVELOPMENT.md). Windows x64 is the only packaged target, and other platforms and GPUs are unverified. See [validation](v0.2.0-alpha.1-validation.md) for the exact checks.
