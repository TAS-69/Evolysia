# Controls and powers

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Pan | WASD, arrows, right-drag or middle-drag |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel, centred on the pointer |
| Inspect | Left-click a person, band or land |
| Pause | Space |
| Speed | 1–6 select 1×, 2×, 3×, 5×, 10×, 25× |
| Overlay | Tab or the top-bar selector |
| Cancel | Esc disarms a power; otherwise it closes the selection |
| Fold a panel | Click its heading or arrow |
| Quick save / save browser | F5 / F9 |
| Follow selected person or band | F; manual panning releases the camera |
| Appearance, saved worlds, settings and model connection | World menu |
| Possession movement | WASD or arrows, one route step per simulated day |
| Possession work | E forage, C chop, Q quarry, R rest, X release |

Chronicle and inspector links jump to the relevant band or person. The planet wraps east-west. Camera zoom ranges from 0.2 to 16; the renderer selects denser art as tiles grow on screen.

The baseline speed is twelve simulated days per second. A year contains 360 days. Model planning can hold time at every speed. Faster settings target multiples of that rate; the actual speed shown in the bar depends on population and rendering cost.

## Intervention

Choose a power in the top bar and click its target. Dreams, curses and possession need a selected living person; possession requires an adult. Dreams offer worship, departure, peace, seeking, courage or love, with optional words. Other powers affect a band or area according to their reach.

| Group | Powers |
|---|---|
| Kindness | Rain, Bless, Fertility, Heal, Game, Grow |
| Harder lessons | Drought, Lightning, Wildfire, Flood, Earthquake, Blizzard, Plague, Meteor, Beasts, Curse |
| Words | Dream, Message |
| Shape the world | Raise land, Lower land |
| Walk among them | Possess |

There are twenty-one powers in current source. Grow restores the native plant/tree stocks within carrying capacity. Raise/lower changes elevation by 200 metres, recalculates drainage and habitats, ruins flooded buildings and makes displaced camps seek nearby dry ground. A raised patch can remain a lake if surrounding drainage traps water.

Possession directs one adult. Select their name, choose Possess, then run time and issue movement or work commands. Fatigue and illness can force rest; chopping and quarrying require basic stone-tool knowledge. Harvested goods enter their real pack, and a person away from camp cannot draw on its food or service providers. X releases them and asks their community to reconsider its plans. The required model remains responsible for the wider world.

Witnessed acts can build awe and recognition. Believers may pray for rain, food, healing, children or protection. Answering a prayer affects belief; ignored prayers can expire and weaken it.

**Message** first unlocks after 250 years, then recharges every 250 years. Leave words on the ground, carve them into a standing stone or send a vision. Ground writing fades after sixty years. People interpret messages through temperament, faith and literacy. Understood text and the chosen interpretation inform fresh model plans; keyword matching does not execute commands in normal gameplay. Dreams influence later planning rather than automatically forcing a migration.
