# Controls and powers

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Pan | WASD, arrows, right-drag or middle-drag |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel, centred on the pointer |
| Inspect | Left-click a person, band or land |
| Pause | Space |
| Speed | 1–6 select 1×, 2×, 3×, 5×, 10×, 25× |
| Overlay | Tab or the top-bar selector |
| Cancel | Esc disarms a power; otherwise it closes the selection |
| Fold a panel | Click its heading or arrow |

Chronicle and inspector links jump to the relevant band or person. The planet wraps east-west. Camera zoom ranges from 0.2 to 16; the renderer selects denser art as tiles grow on screen.

The baseline speed is twelve simulated days per second. A year contains 360 days. Faster settings target multiples of that rate; the actual speed shown in the bar depends on population and rendering cost.

## Intervention

Choose a power in the top bar and click its target. Dreams and curses need a person. Dreams offer worship, departure, peace, seeking, courage or love, with optional words. Other powers affect a band or area according to their reach.

| Group | Powers |
|---|---|
| Kindness | Rain, Bless, Fertility, Heal, Game |
| Harder lessons | Drought, Lightning, Wildfire, Flood, Earthquake, Blizzard, Plague, Meteor, Beasts, Curse |
| Words | Dream, Message |

That is seventeen powers. The art specification also lists future controls such as possession and terrain raising; those are not available in this alpha.

Witnessed acts can build awe and recognition. Believers may pray for rain, food, healing, children or protection. Answering a prayer affects belief; ignored prayers can expire and weaken it.

**Message** first unlocks after 250 years, then recharges every 250 years. Leave words on the ground, carve them into a standing stone or send a vision. Ground writing fades after sixty years. People interpret messages through temperament, faith and literacy; a leader may act on recognised commands. This is bounded interpretation rather than unrestricted natural-language control of the world.
